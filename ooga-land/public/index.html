Welcome to Hazync

Hazync proves that Bitcoin's whole history follows the rules, block by block, from the very first one. The proof is small enough that anyone can check it in a web browser, without downloading the chain and without trusting us.

The work is under way, not finished: the board moved to a new engine on 12.11.2026, restarted from genesis on 16.11.2026, and is proving the chain again from the first block.

This is an independent site, not part of the game. Nothing comes with you from Ooga Booga Land: no account, no character, no inventory.

Things to do here

Pick one. None of them needs an account.

Check a proof yourself

The explorer runs Hazync's verifier inside your browser. Press Check it on this device under the genesis proof: it checks the proof on your own device and reads out the last block it reaches.

Check a proof

Help prove the chain

If you have an NVIDIA graphics card, it can prove blocks for the board. On Windows, the Hazync app does it with no terminal: install it, give the board a name to credit, and press Start. Your name goes on the board next to every block you prove.

Download for Windows Requirements, and Linux

The download is hazync-setup-x86_64-windows.exe on the release page. It is unsigned, so Windows warns the first time; the release's signed SHA256SUMS.txt lists it.

Watch the board

Every block in Bitcoin's history is one square on the block map, filling in as it is proven. The leaderboard shows who proved them.
